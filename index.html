Story

I’ve been fascinated by making videos since primary school, back when I only filmed family videos. The love of creating through the lens has never left me.

For me, filming is a chance to show my best while capturing the most beautiful moments, then shaping them into a memorable film on the editing desk.

Every client gets the best-quality, most beautiful work my skills and equipment can deliver.

Approach

We talk about your day, song or show. I film, staying close to the moment. Then I edit it into a finished film with the pacing and mood it deserves.
